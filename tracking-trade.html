Each stock's expected return follows CAPM from the slider above, so no stock-picking skill is assumed — the spread below is volatility, not a forecast about these particular companies. A real multi-year path also brings takeovers, dilution, regime changes and outright business failure, none of which a lognormal model captures. Read the fan as a rough sense of scale, nothing more.

Stress test — market drawdown

Each holding is moved by its beta × the market fall, plus its own noise scaled to the length of the shock. Two things this cannot capture: in a real crash correlations converge toward 1, so diversification helps less than the model suggests; and beta is measured on ordinary days, then tends to run higher in a panic. Treat these as an optimistic floor, not a worst case. Historical presets are peak-to-trough S&P 500 closes, rounded.
