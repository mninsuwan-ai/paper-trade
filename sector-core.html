Stress test — market drawdown

Each holding is moved by its beta × the market fall, plus its own noise scaled to the length of the shock. Two things this cannot capture: in a real crash correlations converge toward 1, so diversification helps less than the model suggests; and beta is measured on ordinary days, then tends to run higher in a panic.
